How to write a deck for pocket-presenter

Any HTML page works. Each slide is a <section>, your speaker notes go in <aside class="notes"> inside it, and anything with class="fragment" appears one click at a time. reveal.js decks work as they are.

<section>
  <h1>Hello</h1>
  <p class="fragment">Shows on the next click</p>
  <aside class="notes">
    <p>What you say on this slide.</p>
  </aside>
</section>
  • Notes inside a fragment belong to that click, so your phone shows exactly what to say before each click.
  • A <video autoplay> starts when its slide comes up and stops when you move on.
  • Lines in notes that start with [brackets] show as cues, like [pause].
